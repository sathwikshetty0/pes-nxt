# PES NEXT Innovation and Incubation Centre — Master Website Prompt for Kiro

Use this prompt directly in Kiro. It is grounded entirely in real documents from the Centre.

---

## Project Brief

Build a complete, production-ready website for **PES NEXT Innovation and Incubation Centre**, the official incubation and startup ecosystem of PES College of Engineering, Mandya, Karnataka, India. The centre is managed in association with **InUnity Private Limited** and operates under **People's Education Trust (R)**.

---

## Design Direction

- **Style:** Modern startup ecosystem. Clean, minimal, premium.
- **Color palette:** White base, deep blue primary (`#1A2E6F`), orange accent (`#F47C20`), light grey sections (`#F7F8FC`)
- **Typography:** Inter or Plus Jakarta Sans — clean, professional, readable
- **Aesthetic:** Cards with subtle shadows, generous whitespace, smooth scroll, micro-animations on scroll entry
- **Responsive:** Mobile-first. Every section must work flawlessly on mobile, tablet, and desktop
- **Dark mode:** Optional toggle — not required but welcome
- **Tech stack:** Next.js 14, Tailwind CSS, Framer Motion, TypeScript

Do **not** copy generic incubation templates. Every line of copy below is real and sourced from the Centre's official documents.

## PAGE 6 — APPLY FOR INCUBATION

### Who Can Apply

- Students of PES College of Engineering, Mandya
- Alumni entrepreneurs
- Researchers and faculty
- Local entrepreneurs with technology-driven ideas

### What We Look For (Evaluation Criteria)

1. Innovation and technological strength
2. Market opportunity and feasibility
3. Team capability and preparedness
4. Financial and commercial viability
5. Intellectual property potential
6. Alignment with Startup India and Atmanirbhar Bharat

### Application Process

**Stage 1 — Submit Application**

Required documents:
- Executive Summary or Business Plan
- Company Incorporation Documents (if applicable)
- Financial Projections
- Intellectual Property Disclosures (if applicable)

**Stage 2 — Evaluation Presentation**

Shortlisted applicants present before the Evaluation Committee in person or virtually. All submitted information is treated with strict confidentiality.

**Stage 3 — Selection & Onboarding**

Final selection based on presentation quality, business potential, and committee deliberations. Onboarded startups execute an Incubation Agreement.
